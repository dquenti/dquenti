## 01 / About

I'm **Daniela Quenti**, a biochemist and health informatician based in Santiago, Chile.

I lead oncology research projects at **FALP OECI Cancer Center**, where I coordinate research funding, manage clinical data, and help transform health information into evidence for cancer care decisions.

My background connects **molecular biology**, **health informatics**, **clinical research management**, and **open health data**. I am especially interested in how reproducible notebooks, visual analytics, and policy-oriented evidence can make cancer data more useful for researchers, clinicians, and decision-makers.

> Health data has something to say about cancer burden in Latin America.  
> These notebooks make it heard.

<br>

## 02 / What I am building

<table>
<tr>
<td width="58%" valign="top">

### Health in Latin America · Open Data Series 2026

A six-month open notebook series exploring cancer, chronic disease, maternal health, equity, mental health, and child health across Latin America.

Each notebook starts with a public health question and ends with a clear, evidence-based output.

**Format**

Question → Data source → Reproducible analysis → Visual evidence → One-page policy brief

| Open notebooks | 26 |

| Timeframe | 6 months |
| Policy briefs | 1 per notebook |
| Core focus | Evidence for action |

## 03 / Thematic arcs

| Arc | Questions explored |
|---|---|
| **Female cancers** | Breast, cervical, and ovarian cancer burden |
| **Maternal health** | Mortality, access, inequities, and preventable gaps |
| **Non-communicable diseases** | Cardiovascular disease, diabetes, obesity |
| **Mental health** | Burden, care gaps, and COVID-19 legacy |
| **Health equity** | Income, geography, insurance, and access |
| **Child health** | Infant mortality, nutrition, and vaccination |
