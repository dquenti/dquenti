## <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/01-Hi%20there!-7B1557?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=0D1117" alt="Hi there"/>

I'm **Daniela** - a biochemist and health informatician based in Santiago, Chile.

I lead oncology research projects at **FALP OECI Cancer Center**, one of the few OECI-accredited cancer centres in Latin America, where I coordinate research funding, manage clinical data, and generate evidence for cancer care decisions.

My academic path spans molecular biology, a master's in health informatics (Universidad de Chile, **DAAD Fellow**), and research.

> *What brings me here: health data contains insights that should reach further than the spreadsheet they live in.*

## <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/02-What%20I'm%20building-5B4EC8?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=0D1117" alt="What I'm building"/>

<img align="right" width="41%" src="https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Poppins&weight=600&size=17&duration=2300&pause=700&color=E0D4F7&background=0D111700&center=true&vCenter=true&multiline=true&repeat=true&width=430&height=170&lines=Question+%E2%86%92+Data;Analysis+%E2%86%92+Evidence;Notebook+%E2%86%92+Policy+Brief;Evidence+made+visible." alt="Open notebook workflow"/>

**Health in Latin America · Open Data Series**

Open notebooks across 6 months. Each one starts with one question and ends with evidence-based findings - rigorous enough to inform a publication, clear enough for anyone to read.

Every notebook includes a **one-page policy brief**: key findings, evidence summary, and implications for decision-makers.
