### &nbsp; Biochemist · Health Informatician · Data Builder

<br>

I lead oncology research projects at
**[FALP OECI Cancer Center](https://www.falp.cl)** in Santiago —
one of the few OECI-accredited cancer centres in Latin America.

My background spans molecular biology, clinical laboratory work,
and health data infrastructure. I hold a master's in health
informatics from **Universidad de Chile** (DAAD Fellow) and have
co-authored publications in **Nature** journals.

What brings me here: the conviction that health data contains
insights that should reach further than the spreadsheet they
live in.
